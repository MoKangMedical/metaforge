Frequently Asked Questions

What databases does MetaForge connect to?

MetaForge connects to 15+ databases including PubMed, Cochrane Library, Embase, Web of Science, Scopus, CNKI, Wanfang, CINAHL, PsycINFO, ERIC, and more. Starter plans include PubMed and Cochrane; Professional and Enterprise plans include all databases.

Can I export reports in LaTeX format?

Yes. Professional and Enterprise plans support one-click export to LaTeX, PDF, Word, and CSV/JSON. LaTeX output follows journal submission templates and includes properly formatted references.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You can try the statistical workbench with demo data at no cost. The demo includes a complete meta-analysis workflow with forest plots, funnel plots, and bias tests. No registration required.

How does MetaForge ensure data accuracy?

MetaForge uses a dual-validation architecture: AI extraction is cross-checked by rule-based engines. All data points are traceable to source (PMID + page + table). Confidence scores are assigned to each extraction. The system achieves 98%+ accuracy on benchmark datasets.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. You can cancel anytime from your account settings. Your data remains accessible for 30 days after cancellation.
